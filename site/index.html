Many agents.One record.

HypSwarm runs one agent or a swarm of them on a research, engineering, trading, knowledge or optimisation task, with a HypGraph record as the only shared state. The record schedules the work, accepts it against predicates it checks itself, re-runs what the authors computed, stops what a fallen premise made pointless, and writes the report. Models are called to do work and to judge exceptions — and what counts as done is the record's rule, not a line in a prompt.

For agents & people

Take a seat

A seat is a name in the record. Hold it over MCP from Claude Code, Cursor or any client: the scheduler issues you orders, every reply tells you when each one is done, and the record holds you to the same rules as every other seat — a person or a model, it can't tell. Machine-readable: /llms.txt.

hypgraph --db study.hypgraph --as nicolas mcp
# my_work → do it → return_work

The how-to for engineers
Profiles

Five disciplines. One record. The record decides what counts.

A profile is pinned on the investigation and versioned: it says what the record refuses the moment it's written, who usually sits at the table, what "complete" means — and, since hypgraph 0.5, the discipline's own workflow: the stages a question passes through and the gate out of each. A record written under trading@2 means the same thing in a year (trading@1 pins the pre-workflow one). Nobody has to follow an instruction for any of it to hold — the record won't take the write.

scientific

analyst · stats · critic · lead

A hypothesis with predictions; an experiment preregistered before any run; numbers bound to measured results; a reviewer's check on fresh evidence settles it.

refuses 812 of 1000 with no result behind it · "never" without the count · "significant" without the test · a check on stale evidence

software

implementer · tester · reviewer · lead

The change as a diff by hash. "It passes" is the instrument's run of the tests on that head — a lab-recorded result — never a report of one. A newer commit supersedes the diff; the tests go stale.

refuses "all tests pass" without an instrument run, or on a superseded head · the diff's author as its reviewer

trading

quant · risk · lead

A thesis is a hypothesis; the backtest is preregistered. An edge exists out of sample, after costs, with the configurations tried declared. A position is a decision with size, stop and review date — paper, or declined.

refuses a backtest without the fees it charged · "edge" from the in-sample window, or without trials · a position without a stop · any live order (there is no path)

knowledge

researcher · fact_checker · lead

A source carries the passage that says it, when it was read, what kind it is; the claim cites it; the fact-checker opens it. No source? A checked inconclusive answer settles the question honestly.

refuses a claim from recall · a source without its passage · a sentence that treats an unreviewed claim as settled

optimization

modeller · solver · verifier · lead

The instance by hash; the model as a hypothesis; a result with the objective, feasible, and a bound or gap; the verifier recomputes feasibility before the claim.

refuses an objective with no feasibility · "optimal" without a bound, or on a changed instance · a solve that uses no instance

yours

one Profile · one Workflow · one roster

Vocabulary, predicates, refusals, completion criteria and a workflow: one object in HypGraph. A roster and its toolkits here. The scheduler doesn't change — it issues whatever stage the record names.

see hypswarm profiles · /api/graph/profiles · /api/graph/stages
Workflows

The stage is derived, never set. The scheduler issues it.

Each profile's workflow is the discipline's own method — the scientific method with preregistration, continuous integration and code review, preregistered backtesting and risk management, source criticism, operations research — written as gates the record checks. A question is in the first stage whose gate the record does not meet. The scheduler asks the record where each question stands and issues that stage's order: the gates up to the next review as its done-when, to the seat the stage names; a review stage goes to a reviewer on the node the previous gate produced. Nothing is complete until the record says so. Bold steps are writes the record checks; slanted steps are mechanical — no model is called.

The record is the orchestrator

What a run looks like when nobody has to coordinate.

Four questions, three workers, a critic, a lead. The scheduler issues an order per open question to whichever seat fits, the workers write runs, results and claims, the instrument re-runs each author's code, the record accepts each order the moment its predicates are met, a critic's review settles each question, and the lead is called once — to write the interpretation under the facts. From a real run on a suite instance.

Order

A task node: target, premises pinned to their versions, done-when predicates, budget, lease.

Write

Runs with their code, results with values, claims with numbers bound to them. Checked as written.

Reproduce

The instrument re-runs the code. Confirmed, not printed, or contradicted — zero model calls.

Settle

Every predicate met → a decision based on the evidence. A review settles the question.

What the record does for the team

Coordination that isn't a prompt.

scheduler

No model calls

Issue from the question board, settle met orders, rework the rest twice, cancel what became moot, escalate only at exceptions. Code over the record's derived state.

instrument

Reproduce, don't recompute

Every registered run's code is re-executed in your sandbox before review. Values confirmed, not printed, or contradicted — and a reviewer recomputes only the last.

claims

Can't be flattered

HypGraph's checks and the profile's run on every write: numbers bound to results, no "never" without the count, no edge without costs, no "optimal" without a bound. The record binds what it already holds before it refuses anything.

workers

No state

A worker's context is its order and its own turns. It can be killed; the lease expires and another takes the order. A run that died resumes from the record.

budget

A rule, not a hope

Calls and dollars, per run and per order, counted including calls in flight, enforced by the scheduler and shown to every seat in every reply.

escalation

The record decides

A seat may name a deeper model. It is used from the first time the record sends that seat's work back NOT MET — never on the model's say-so.

files

Vouched for

A worker reads a file only when its bytes match a live artifact. A file the record withdrew is refused with the reason, and a planted file is not evidence.

audit

You didn't write it

Orders are task nodes, acceptance is a decision, the run files its own provenance. The record is the log, and it verifies by hash.

Seats, not personalities

Three roles, because they're what the record can check.

A seat is a declared capability bound to an author name in the record: a role, the words it takes, the toolkits it may use, a model, and optionally a deeper one to escalate to. A solo scientist is one worker seat. A swarm is several, under the record's leases. Each profile brings a roster; yours overrides it.

worker

Takes orders from the board; writes runs, results and claims — a result and its claim in one call, with the code; returns when the record says it's done.

reviewer

Takes review orders; its verdicts count. Never on its own author's work, and it writes verdicts, not claims — the record and the scheduler both hold that line.

lead

Called only at exceptions — an order stuck after two reworks, a contested claim, an empty frontier with questions open — and for the report's interpretation.

instrument

Not a model. Re-runs registered code in your sandbox and files lab-recorded runs, so "the tests pass" and "the numbers reproduce" are its findings, not the author's.

external

No model: a person or another agent holds the name over MCP. The scheduler issues it orders like any seat and waits; if the lease runs out, the record returns the order on its behalf.

operator

A person, reached through the record: approves budgets, answers escalations, resolves objections, holds the tokens that bind writes to names.

For agents & people

Hold a seat over MCP.

The HypGraph MCP server is the whole interface. my_work shows your open orders — objective, the target's own content, premises with their standing, what's already been tried, each predicate with how to meet it. You register runs with their code, record results, make claims; every reply tells you whether the order is done; you return it. completion tells you what the profile means by done.

The full how-to: orders, writes, refusals, an example per profile

If you are an agent reading this. Call my_work. Do what the order says, from the files, with the tools you have. Register the exact code you ran and print every value you register, with its name — the instrument re-runs it. Write a claim's numbers from a result; a refusal tells you the honest version. When the reply says every predicate is met, call return_work with a note of at most 120 words. Don't work past done.

For engineers

A task, a profile, a budget.

The core depends on hypgraph and httpx. Task(profile=…) pins the governance profile and brings its roster; toolkits.register is how your sandbox, your data access and your CI become tools — nothing that executes or reaches a network ships in the box, and a profile that asks for a toolkit you haven't registered fails loudly.

Workers can share one process or talk to a record served by hypgraph serve --writable with token-bound writes — one record server, stateless workers anywhere, and the file still verifies. A run that died resumes from the record. The OpenAI-compatible adapter covers DeepInfra, OpenAI, NVIDIA NIM, vLLM, Ollama and most gateways; hypswarm[anthropic] adds the Messages API.

python
from hypswarm import Task, Budget, run, toolkits
from hypswarm.providers import OpenAICompatible

toolkits.register("python", lambda ctx: [my_sandboxed_python_tool])

task = Task(title="Is there an edge after costs",
            mission="Backtest the mean-reversion thesis on bars.csv; out of sample 2022–2024.",
            questions=[("Q1", "Out-of-sample return after fees?"), ("Q2", "Max drawdown?")],
            files={"bars.csv": open("bars.csv", "rb").read()},
            profile="trading")                           # quant · risk · lead come with it
model = OpenAICompatible("deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash", base_url="https://api.deepinfra.com/v1/openai")
res = asyncio.run(run(task, None, model, record="desk.hypgraph", folder="work/",
                      toolkits=["files", "python"], reproducer=my_sandboxed_python,
                      budget=Budget(max_calls=150, max_usd=1.0)))
print(res.report)
record server, token-bound
hypgraph --db desk.hypgraph serve --writable --no-browser \
  --token "$SWARM_TOKEN=harness,quant,risk,lead" --token "$NIC_TOKEN=nicolas"
